## What the user asked
On Me, people can edit their photo, name, city and birthday. Family members see each other's photos wherever an avatar shows, and can tap a member to see a simple profile with their photo, name, city + local time and birthday.

Rulings applied: (1) `ios/Shared/**` may be edited; (2) every share bar uses the accent colour; (3) the stored timezone updates quietly when the app becomes active and the phone's zone differs.
## Review fixes (second opinion: handoffs/reviews/2026-11-15-1814-profile-ios-second.md): done
1. **The photo upload is tied to its session (blocking fix).**
   - `ConvexSession.currentGeneration` exposes the generation that every sign-in, sign-out and forget bumps.
   - `ConvexAuthService.setPhoto` notes the generation and the session's `userId` at the start; with no session it throws `.notAuthenticated`.
   - After the upload and again after `setAvatar`, `ensureSameSession` checks that the generation and user are unchanged and a session is still stored. If not, it throws `CancellationError`, and `setAvatar` never runs for the next account.
   - `SessionStore` has an `accountEpoch`, bumped in `start`, `signIn`, `signOut` and `deleteAccount`. `saveProfile`, `setPhoto` and `removePhoto` go through `applyToSameAccount`: if the epoch or `user?.id` changed while they ran, it throws `CancellationError` and leaves `user` alone.
   - `ProfileEditView.message(for:)` returns nil for `CancellationError`, so no notice shows.
2. **Birthdays are always Gregorian.** `ProfileFormat.birthdayCalendar()` is Gregorian in the phone's time zone. It is now the default for `birthdayString`, `pickerDate` and `isValidBirthday`, and the edit screen uses it for `birthdayRange`, `defaultBirthday` and the wheel (`.environment(\.calendar, …)`). A phone set to the Buddhist or Japanese calendar now stores 1990, not 2533 or Heisei 2.
3. **Very narrow photos work.**
   - `ProfilePhoto.scaledSize` keeps both sides at 1 px or more (9999×1 → 512×1).
   - `downscaled` now returns `UIImage?` and gives nil for a zero or non-finite size, so `jpeg(from:)` does too.

## Review fixes, round 2 (brief: handoffs/briefs/2026-11-15-1843-profile-photo-account-guard.md): done
Closes both findings in `handoffs/reviews/2026-11-15-1814-profile-ios-second.md` with one server-side guard. A photo picked by account A can't land on account B, whatever the sign-out/sign-in timing.
1. **Server guard.** `users:setAvatar` now takes `{ storageId, userId }` and `users:removeAvatar` takes `{ userId }`. Right after `requireUser`, if the caller isn't `userId` the server returns `fail("not_authorized")`. That happens before any read, patch or delete. The ownership, size and type checks are unchanged. `not_authorized` is a new code in `convex/lib/errors.ts`. `not_authenticated` would have been the closest existing code, but the app signs out on it, so it would have signed B out.
2. **The account is noted when the action starts.** In `ProfileEditView`, `upload` (PhotosPicker change and camera result) and `removePhoto` read `session.user?.id` synchronously before starting the task, so before `loadTransferable` or the downscale. The id is passed through `SessionStore.setPhoto(jpeg:userId:)` / `removePhoto(userId:)` → `AuthService.setPhoto(jpeg:userId:)` / `removePhoto(userId:)` → Convex `setAvatar({storageId, userId})` / `removeAvatar({userId})`.
   - `SessionStore` throws `CancellationError` up front when `user?.id` is no longer that id. This covers finding 1: a late picker load after B signs in sends nothing.
   - `ConvexAuthService` checks the passed id against the session before the upload URL is fetched, then again around `setAvatar` and `removeAvatar`. The generation checks are unchanged.
   - The server compares the id against whichever token sends the request. So an auth retry that runs as B is rejected (finding 2). `ConvexAuthService` maps that `not_authorized` to `CancellationError`, so no notice shows.
   - `MockAuthService` applies the same check against the mock's signed-in user.
3. **New tests.**
   - Backend `profile.test.ts`: "setAvatar and removeAvatar refuse a userId that isn't the caller's, changing nothing". B sends A's id; both mutations fail with `not_authorized`; B's photo and blob stay; A stays without a photo. The existing tests now pass `userId`.
   - iOS `TimeZoneSyncTests.photoPickedAsOneAccountIsNotSentForTheNext`: the account is noted as u1, the "load" is held by a `TestGate`, the app signs out and signs in as u2, then the gate opens. Result: `CancellationError`, no `setPhoto` call on the auth service, and the same for `removePhoto`. The user is still u2.
   - iOS `ConvexProfileTests.setPhotoUploadsThenSetsAvatar` now expects `setAvatar` args `{storageId, userId: "user_1"}`, and `removePhotoAndTimeZone` expects `removeAvatar` args `{userId: "user_1"}`.
   - iOS `ConvexProfileTests.serverRefusalForAnotherAccountIsACancellation`: `not_authorized` from setAvatar/removeAvatar → `CancellationError`.
   - iOS `ConvexProfileTests.photoForAnotherAccountIsNeverSent`: a userId that isn't the session's → `CancellationError`, and no avatar calls go out.

## What changed
- **Me** has a profile tile showing the photo (or initial), name, city ("Sydney") and "Birthday 12 March", plus an **Edit profile** row.
- **Edit profile** is one screen:
  - Photo: Choose a photo (PhotosPicker, so no library permission), Take a photo (system camera, only shown when the phone has one) and Remove photo. A photo change saves straight away, with a Thinking Orb and "Adding your photo…" while it uploads. Photos are made upright, scaled to at most 512 px and saved as JPEG at 0.8 before upload.
  - Name, plus "Lives in" (typed city, 40 letters max, empty clears it).
  - Birthday: "Add your birthday" opens a wheel picker limited to 1900 to today. The set date shows with a Change option, and "Remove birthday" clears it.
  - Save is at the bottom and shows the saving orb.
  - Errors show as a calm `Notice`. The new codes `invalid_photo`, `invalid_city` and `invalid_birthday` have their own gentle messages: "That photo didn't work. Please try a different one." / "Please keep the place to 40 letters or fewer." / "Please pick a birthday that's already happened."
- **Member profile** opens as a sheet when you tap a member on Home ("Everyone today") or in Family settings ("People"). It shows:
  - a big photo, the name and "Organiser" when it applies
  - "Lives in": "Kathmandu · 3:11 pm" (ticks every minute). With no or unknown timezone it shows the city only; with no city, "9:41 pm local time".
  - "Birthday": "24 August" (never the year)
  - if there's nothing to show, one plain line saying they haven't added it yet
- **Avatars are photos only.** One shared `Avatar` (`Components/Card.swift`) loads the photo with AsyncImage. With no photo, while loading, or on failure, it shows the initial on the neutral `surfaceMuted` fill with `secondaryText`. `avatarColor` is gone from the app: removed from `AppUser`, `FamilyMember` and `FeedActor` (the JSON key is now ignored), from `ActivityStyle` (deleted) and from `Color(hex:)` (deleted). Feed avatars, the Home avatar stack, member rows, Family settings and Me all use it. The share bars are all the accent colour.
- **Time zone follow-up:** `SessionStore.syncTimeZone()` runs when the app becomes active and when a user appears. If `TimeZone.current.identifier` differs from the stored one, it calls `updateProfile({timezone})` with no UI and ignores failures.
- **Services** follow the protocol/mock split. `AuthService` gains `updateProfile(ProfileUpdate)`, `setPhoto(jpeg:)`, `removePhoto()` and `updateTimeZone(_:)`.
  - Convex: `updateProfile` always sends `name`, `city` and `birthday` (string or `null`; a new `ConvexArg.null` encodes as JSON null, as the SDK does for nil). `setPhoto` runs `generateAvatarUploadUrl`, then a plain URLSession `POST` with `Content-Type: image/jpeg` (`URLSessionPhotoUploader`, injectable as `PhotoUploading` for tests), then `setAvatar({storageId})`, then `me`. Each of these finishes by reading `me`.
  - Mock: it validates like the backend, stores photos as temp-file URLs and seeds profiles: Priya (Sydney, 12 Mar), Aama (Kathmandu, 24 Aug, sample photo), Buwa (Pokhara), Rohan (Melbourne, Australia/Melbourne zone).
- Delete account now lists "Your name, photo and sign-in".
- Debug launch screens `-screen profile-edit` and `-screen member` (Aama) were added for screenshots.
