## Redo (review fixes, handoffs/reviews/2026-11-14-1510-convex-push-second.md)
1. **Evening nudge: once per person per Nepal day, about their family furthest from goal.** `internal.notifications.eveningNudge` now pages through opted-in people (`notificationPrefs` where `eveningNudge` is true, new index `by_evening_nudge`, 100 per page), not through families. For each person it looks at all their families, skips those at or over goal, and nudges once about the one with the lowest share of its goal. Ties go to the family with more steps left. Because it pages by person, someone whose families would have landed on different pages still gets one nudge. The new optional `notificationPrefs.lastNudgeDay` is set when the nudge is sent, so a second run on the same Nepal day sends nothing. Family totals are cached for the length of a page. This depends on `eveningNudge` defaulting to false: people with no prefs row haven't opted in, so they're never paged.
2. **Device cap: 10 per user.** `registerDevice` keeps the phone being registered plus that user's 9 most recently registered others, and deletes the rest. This uses the new index `devices.by_user_updated`. Re-registering a phone refreshes it, and a token moved from another user counts toward the new owner's cap. `push.devicesFor` now reads all of a user's devices, at most 10 thanks to the cap, so every registered phone gets the push. `MAX_DEVICES_PER_USER = 10` moved to `lib/constants.ts`.

## What changed
- **walk_started is once per walk.** `postEvent` skips the uniqueness check for `walk_started`, so 3 walks give 3 events in each family. Each walk also pushes. `member_*` and `family_*` stay at once per (family, actor, day, type).
- **Devices** (`notifications.registerDevice` / `unregisterDevice`):
  - Tokens are trimmed and lowercased, and must be even-length hex of 16–400 characters. Anything else gives the new error `invalid_device_token`.
  - Registering a token that another user had moves it to the caller.
  - `unregisterDevice` deletes the token only if it belongs to the caller. Otherwise it does nothing.
- **Prefs:** `notificationPrefs()` returns the defaults (true, true, true, false) until the user saves a change. `setNotificationPrefs({walkStarted?, goalHit?, reactions?, eveningNudge?})` changes only the fields sent and returns all four.
- **Who gets what** (`lib/push.ts`). Mutations only choose recipients and copy, then call `ctx.scheduler.runAfter(0, internal.pushNode.send, …)`.
  - **walk_started:** every member of each of the walker's families except the walker, with duplicates removed so each person gets one push. Members who turned off `walkStarted` are skipped. A person who shares several families with the walker is sent to the first shared family and that family's event. Copy: "Aama started a walk" / "Want to join? Give them a call." A walker with no name shows as "Someone in your family".
  - **family_100:** every member who has `goalHit` on, sent once, at the moment the event is created. Copy: "<Family> reached today's goal" / "Every step counted. Well walked, everyone."
  - **Reaction:** goes to the event's actor when a reaction is added or replaced, not when it's removed. Nothing is sent for your own reaction, for family events (no actor), or when the actor has `reactions` off. At most 1 push per event per 10 minutes, tracked by a new `feedEvents.lastReactionPushAt` field. Copy: "Buwa reacted 👏 to your 5,000 steps" (or "your 10,000 steps" / "your walk") / "In <Family>."
  - **Evening nudge:** `internal.notifications.eveningNudge` runs from `crons.cron("15 13 * * *")`, which is 19:00 Nepal. It goes once per opted-in person, about their family furthest from goal (see Redo). The copy is: "An evening walk?" / "Your family is at 62% today. A short evening walk would help everyone get there." The percentage is rounded down, so 99.9% shows 99%. When the family has no steps yet the body is "Your family hasn't logged steps yet today. A short evening walk would be a lovely start." No member's name is ever used.
- **Payload:** `{aps: {alert: {title, body}, sound: "default"}, type, familyId, eventId?}`. `type` is one of `walk_started`, `family_100`, `reaction` or `evening_nudge`. The nudge has no `eventId`.
- **Delivery** (`pushNode.send`, `"use node"`, with its logic in `lib/apns.ts`):
  - Sends over `node:http2`, one connection per APNs host per batch. A device's environment picks `api.push.apple.com` (production) or `api.sandbox.push.apple.com` (sandbox).
  - Headers: `authorization: bearer <ES256 JWT>` (kid = `APNS_KEY_ID`, iss = `APPLE_TEAM_ID`), `apns-topic` = `APPLE_BUNDLE_ID` or `com.bixustudio.hamrosteps` when unset, `apns-push-type: alert`, `apns-priority: 10`.
  - The JWT is signed with `jose` and reused for 50 minutes. A 403 `ExpiredProviderToken` / `InvalidProviderToken` clears the cache.
  - A 410, or a 400 with `BadDeviceToken` / `Unregistered`, deletes the device. A 410 with a timestamp keeps a token that was registered again after that time.
  - A network error counts as failed and keeps the device. When the APNs env is missing it logs once and skips. A malformed key logs and skips.
  - The transport sits behind `ApnsTransport`, which tests replace with `setApnsTransportForTests`.
- **Cleanup:** account deletion removes the user's devices and prefs (`deleteUserPush`, at the spot marked in `lib/cleanup.ts`). Devices and prefs belong to a user, not a family, so leaving a family doesn't touch them.

**Schema:**
- New table `devices`: apnsToken, userId, environment (`"sandbox" | "production"`), updatedAt. Indexes `by_token`, `by_user`.
- New table `devices` also has index `by_user_updated` (redo).
- New table `notificationPrefs`: userId plus the four booleans, plus optional `lastNudgeDay` (redo). Indexes `by_user`, `by_evening_nudge`. No row means the defaults.
- New optional field on `feedEvents`: `lastReactionPushAt`.
