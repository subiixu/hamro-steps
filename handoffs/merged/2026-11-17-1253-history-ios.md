## What the user asked
T-44 (part 2 of 2): tapping Home's "This week" card opens a History screen with two tabs, Family and Me. Each shows daily steps for 7 / 30 / 90 days as a bar chart, with a few plain-words trend lines underneath. It shows only the family total and your own steps, never another member's.

## What changed
- **Home**: the "This week" card now has a "See history ›" line and opens History when tapped. It uses a NavigationLink push, with an accessibility hint. The rest of Home is unchanged.
- **History screen**: two rows of large pill buttons, Family | Me and 7 / 30 / 90 days. When text is too big to fit, they stack instead of being cut off (ViewThatFits).
  - The bar chart uses Swift Charts. The x axis uses Nepal-day strings rather than dates, so bars never shift with the phone's time zone.
  - Family tab: days that hit the goal are in the green `goal` colour, other days in a soft accent tint, and a dashed "Goal 50k" line marks the goal. Me tab: plain accent bars. Nothing is ever red.
  - Days before the family existed are left out.
  - Pull to refresh reloads. Loading shows the Thinking Orb (`OrbLoader`).
  - Problems use the existing neutral `Notice` with "Try again".
- **Trend card ("How it's going")**: "Goal reached on X of N days" (Family only), "Daily average 48,200", "Up 12% / Down 8% on the previous 7 days" or "About the same as the previous 7 days" (within ±3%), and "Best day: Sat 14 Nov · 61,400".
  - The comparison line is left out when the earlier period has no data.
  - Today isn't finished yet, so it's left out of the daily average and the comparison. It still counts toward goal days and best day. Without this, every morning would read "Down".
  - If two days tie for best, the most recent one wins. Days with zero steps never count as a best day.
- **Me tab**: reads HealthKit on the phone through `StepSync.source` (`dailyStepTotals`), 2 × N days bucketed by Nepal day. There's no backend call.
  - Days before Health's first steps in the window count as "no data", so a new phone doesn't drag the average down.
  - If Health access is missing, it shows a short sentence instead of an error. There are three cases: Health not on the device, access never asked for, or no steps found. The card also says "From Apple Health on this phone. Only you see this."
- **Service**: `FamilyHistory` / `FamilyHistoryDay` models, and `familyHistory(familyId:days:)` on the protocol, Convex (`home:familyHistory`, `days` sent as a number), the mock, and the Debug `HomeStateFamily` wrapper.
  - The mock follows the backend rules: snapshot days use the frozen goal, today is live, and before-family days are flagged.
  - The sample families now have about 60 days of varied, slowly rising history, so 30 and 90 days look realistic. A 90-day view starts when the family did and has no comparison line.

## Notes for Nayo
- **Protocol default**: `FamilyService.swift` has an `extension FamilyService` default for `familyHistory` that throws `.unavailable`. It exists only because two test stubs outside my file scope also conform: `StubFamilyService` in `HamroStepsTests/TestSupport.swift`, and the private `PinnedFamilyService` in `HomeDayWatchTests.swift`. Convex, the mock and `HomeStateFamily` all implement it explicitly. If you'd rather not have the default, add a one-line pass-through to those two stubs and delete the extension.
- **Colour decision**: the brief said "hit days use the accent colour". I used the app's `goal` green for hit days instead, to match Home's week markers where green means goal reached, and a soft accent tint for other days. The Me tab is plain accent. Switching to accent is a one-line change in `HistoryChart.color(_:)`.
- **Wording**: the Me loading message reuses `OrbLoader`'s "Connecting to Apple Health…" because `OrbLoader.swift` was outside scope. A dedicated "Reading your steps…" activity would fit better later.
- `ios/project.yml` is untouched: the new folder is picked up from the `HamroSteps` source path, and Charts is a system framework that links automatically.
- **Release**: no data changes on the phone. The screen works fully on the mock. On Convex the Family tab shows "can't load" until the backend's `home:familyHistory` is deployed, so ship the backend first or together.
