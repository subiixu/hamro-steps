# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (8) · 2026-11-17
- See how your family has been walking. Tap "This week" on Home to open History: your family's daily steps against the goal over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, with how many days you reached it, your daily average, and whether you're up or down on the period before.
- Switch to "Me" to see your own daily steps from Apple Health. Only you see them.

## 0.1.0 (7) · 2026-11-15
- A fresh look, a new app icon, and profiles: add a photo, the city you live in and your birthday, and tap anyone on Home to see theirs with their local time.
