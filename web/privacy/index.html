Hamro Steps

Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version

Hamro Steps helps families see each other's daily steps. We use your step count for that and nothing else. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we read from your phone

  • Apple Health step count. The app only reads it. It never writes to Health. Your step data is never used for advertising and is never sold.
  • Push notifications. If you allow them, we send alerts such as "a family member started a walk". They are delivered through Apple's notification service.

We ask for no other permissions. The app does not use your location.

What your family can see

Only people in your family can see:

  • your daily step total
  • when you start and stop a walk
  • your status
  • reactions you send and receive

Nothing else from Health is shared.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Convex, the service that runs our backend and acts as our data processor. It holds your family, your membership and your daily step totals.

Leaving and deleting

  • Leave a family: your shared data is removed from that family.
  • Delete your account: everything we hold about you is removed.

Questions or requests about your data: bixustudio@gmail.com
